## Important corrections

1. Lesson 2 now explicitly preserves `vara`, `kunna`, sentence types, gender, articles, demonstrative construction and personal pronouns.
2. Lesson 3 separates calendar/time, phrasal verbs, word order and numerals.
3. Lesson 4 restores the article/declension/adjective sequence.
4. Lesson 5 restores numerals from 21 and adjective forms.
5. Lesson 6 restores `bruka`, genitive, `jätte-`, object pronouns and modal verbs.
6. Lesson 7 restores definiteness, `som`, `sådan`, `annan`, negative word formation and `se ut`.
7. Lesson 8 restores the `sig/sin` block and distinguishes it from the preterite/supine block.
8. Lesson 9 assigns short answers to general questions to Unit 9.4 and keeps `någon/ingen` in 9.3.
9. Lesson 10 restores `skal`, adjective agreement, qualitative adverbs and indeclinable adjectives.
10. Lesson 11 restores `man`, impersonal constructions and `kunna/veta/känna`.
11. Lesson 12 restores the complete pronoun/quantity/conjunction/adverb block.
12. Lesson 13 restores adjective derivation with `-ad`.
13. Lesson 14 restores substantive adjectives, comparative adverbs and `lagom`.
14. Lesson 15 restores theatre/cultural content, participle I functions and `det är ... som`.
15. Lesson 16 restores Sweden, participle II/passive and `tro/tycka/tänka`.
16. Lesson 17 restores participle II with particles and `fort/bara`.
17. Lesson 18 separates perfect infinitive (18.3) from preterite/pluperfect unreality and `skulle` (18.4).

## Integrity note

The Unit/LCP/CP architecture is project design. The source-derived structure map is the canonical bridge between the scanned book and the project route. This audit does not claim that the book itself defines the project's Unit boundaries.

Full page-by-page verification of Lessons 2–18 remains a separate evidence task; no unsupported source detail has been invented in this alignment pass.
