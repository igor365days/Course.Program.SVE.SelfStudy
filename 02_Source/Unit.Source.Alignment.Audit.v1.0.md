## Completed page-verification batches

### Lesson 1
Previously verified directly from source pages 6–15.

### Lesson 2
Pages 16–26 were inspected directly. The four project Units were confirmed against the relevant blocks:

- 2.1: greetings, good wishes, introductions, personal-information questions and basic conversation.
- 2.2: personal/non-personal verb forms, four conjugations, infinitive/present formation, `vara`, `kunna`.
- 2.3: sentence members, sentence types by communicative purpose and composition, impersonal and indefinite-personal constructions.
- 2.4: word order, questions, adverbial position, noun gender, articles, demonstrative construction and personal pronouns.

### Lesson 3
Pages 27–38 were inspected directly. The four project Units were confirmed against the relevant blocks:

- 3.1: time, parts of day, days of week, time intervals, frequency, seasons and months; reinforced by `Min arbetsdag` and exercises.
- 3.2: post-verbal particles and compound verbal predicate.
- 3.3: direct/inverted word order, question word order and placement of phrasal adverbials, including constructions with post-verbal particles.
- 3.4: cardinal numbers 0–20 and detailed time-telling models.

## Important corrections preserved from the alignment pass

1. Lesson 2 explicitly preserves `vara`, `kunna`, sentence types, gender, articles, demonstrative construction and personal pronouns.
2. Lesson 3 separates calendar/time, phrasal verbs, word order and numerals.
3. Later lessons retain the corrected source-map allocations from the initial 56-Unit alignment pass.

## Integrity note

The Unit/LCP/CP architecture is project design. The source-derived structure map is the canonical bridge between the scanned book and the project route. Direct page verification establishes evidence for canonical content; it does not imply that the book itself defines our Unit boundaries.

The current PDF is image-based. Verification is therefore performed by direct inspection of rendered source pages rather than relying on incomplete automated text extraction.
