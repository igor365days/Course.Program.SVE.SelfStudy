## Important distinction

`SOURCE_SUPPORTED` means that the underlying source content is available through the verified Unit/LCP chain. It does not certify that the CP assessment design is pedagogically optimal.

CP content is project-added and must remain distinguishable from book-derived content.

## Required CP semantic audit

For each CP:

1. Confirm that every preceding LCP contributes evidence to the CP.
2. Confirm that the CP does not introduce ungrounded source content.
3. Confirm that the CP tests integration rather than duplicating one Unit exercise.
4. Confirm remediation logic and transition to the next Section.
5. Confirm evidence/verification rules.
