## Important distinction

`SOURCE_SUPPORTED` does **not** mean that the LCP assessment design is pedagogically optimal. It means only that its underlying Unit content can be traced to the available source evidence.

The LCP assessment design itself is project-added material and must be evaluated against the course assessment standard separately.

## Required LCP semantic audit

For each LCP:

1. Every covered Unit must be represented by at least one meaningful checkpoint.
2. The checkpoint must test the Unit's canonical objective, not merely vocabulary recall unless vocabulary recall is the Unit objective.
3. No assessment requirement may introduce source-derived grammar that is absent from the covered Units.
4. Project-added communicative tasks must be identifiable as project design.
5. Verification must respect `Assessment.Verification.Policy.md`.
6. The transition to the next Lesson must be explicit.
