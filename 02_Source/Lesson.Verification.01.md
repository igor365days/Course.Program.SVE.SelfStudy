**Source pages:** 6–15  
**Status:** Verified at source-structure level

## Verification basis

Проверены страницы 6–15 исходной книги и сопоставлены с Unit 1.1–1.5 текущего курса.

## Source structure

The source lesson contains these major blocks:

1. Основные правила произношения и обозначения шведских звуков.
2. Шведский алфавит.
3. Понятие о тоническом ударении.
4. Интонация в простых повествовательных предложениях.
5. Интонация в вопросительных предложениях.

The first block also contains the detailed treatment of Swedish consonants, vowels, length/shortness and specific consonant/letter combinations. The alphabet block contains the full alphabet table and the special letters å, ä, ö.

## Corrections made

The existing Unit definitions were too generic in several places and did not explicitly preserve the source scope. They were revised to:

- identify source page ranges;
- distinguish the alphabet from the phonetic treatment;
- explicitly include the specific consonant/letter combinations presented in the source;
- explicitly describe the two types of tonic accent;
- explicitly distinguish narrative, general-question and special-question intonation.
